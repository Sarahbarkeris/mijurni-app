💡 mijurni safety tips — Ibiza
🚑
Ambulance tip — important: If an ambulance is called, always ask to be taken to a public hospital (Hospital Can Misses). Private hospitals may not be covered by your travel insurance and bills can reach thousands of pounds.
💊
Drink spiking: Ibiza has higher than average incidents near clubs. Never leave drinks unattended. Use the buddy system on big nights — nobody walks home alone.
🚕
Taxis: Only use white licensed taxis or the Taxis Ibiza app. Unlicensed drivers near clubs routinely charge 5–10x the normal fare.
☀️
Heat & dehydration: August hits 35°C+. Dehydration is the most common reason groups end up in hospital. Drink water between every alcoholic drink. Beach time before 11am or after 4pm.
💳
ATMs: Use ATMs inside supermarkets or banks only. Standalone ATMs near clubs have high card skimming rates. Tell your bank you're travelling before you go.
📱
Lost phone plan: Before your first night out, screenshot the hotel address in Spanish and save it offline. If your phone dies you can show a taxi driver exactly where to go.
🛡️
Travel insurance: Check your policy covers water sports, nightlife and medical evacuation before you travel. Many standard policies exclude activities.
🌊
Sea & boats: Ibiza currents can be strong around cliffs. On boat trips — always wear a life jacket if provided. Never dive from heights after drinking.
